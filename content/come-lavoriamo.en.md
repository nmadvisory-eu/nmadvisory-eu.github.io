## What does Operating Intelligence mean?

OPINT stands for Operating Intelligence. We do not deal with strategy in general terms: our ground is operations, meaning the way strategies and projects become processes, systems, organisation and measurable results.

## How do we start?

With a short conversation, to understand your situation. Then, usually, with one of these three first steps, each with scope, schedule and results defined at the outset:

{{< schede >}}
{{< scheda icona="treestructure" titolo="Process diagnostics" link="/en/process-diagnostics/" >}}In a few weeks, a shared base of facts on processes, costs, organisation and systems{{< /scheda >}}
{{< scheda icona="coins" titolo="Indirect cost review" link="/en/indirect-costs/" >}}Savings opportunities on telecommunications, IT, services and materials, based on real needs and data{{< /scheda >}}
{{< scheda icona="arrowsmerge" titolo="Operational and IT due diligence" link="/en/transformation-growth/post-merger-carve-out/" >}}Before an acquisition: systems, processes, contracts, risks and hidden costs{{< /scheda >}}
{{< /schede >}}

Further proposals come out of the first step, if and when they are needed: with clear objectives, timing and effort. You decide whether and how to continue, and the results stay with you, usable on your own as well.

## How do we work with your team?

With people, not over their heads. We make the most of informal know-how, often ignored in top-down projects, and leave methods and tools with your team. We use a deliberately simple notation, because results must be understood by those who do the work and those who lead, not only by specialists.

## What do you get working with us?

- **The people you meet are the people who do the work**
- **Management experience**, accountable for results and not only for advice
- **From analysis to execution**, with agreed objectives
- **Concrete, low-intrusion tools**, and knowledge that stays with your team
- **Independence**: we do not sell software and have no agreements with those who do

## How much of your people's time does it take?

Little, and it is stated at the start. The intensive work is ours: your people take part in focused sessions and validate the results. In process diagnostics, for example, it usually takes 8 to 24 hours per key contact over the whole project. In critical phases, when needed, we are there in person.

## How do we use artificial intelligence?

As a tool, never as a source. It helps us read documents, data and flows faster and speeds up documentation. We check the content, and you validate it.

## Do you work with other specialists?

Yes, when needed. We coordinate specialised partners, for example legal, financial or technology experts. We can also take part in selecting the talent to bring into the organisation, or take on interim roles for a limited period.
