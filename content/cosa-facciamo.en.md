## How do they fit together?

Diagnostics is often the common starting point, because it gives everyone a shared base of facts. From there, indirect cost optimisation frees up resources quickly, work on processes and technology makes improvements last, and strategic transformation changes the company's course.

An example: after an acquisition, diagnostics shows where the two ways of working differ; the indirect cost review brings duplicate suppliers and contracts together; integrating processes and systems makes the result stick.

But each area also stands on its own: start with the one that addresses the problem you have today.
