## When does it help?

{{< spunta >}}
- You are considering an acquisition and want to know the target's systems, processes and risks
- You have just closed an acquisition and the synergies must arrive as promised
- You grow through a series of acquisitions and need a repeatable method
- You are selling part of the company or a product line
- A company owned by funds is preparing for the investor's exit
{{< /spunta >}}

## Which levers do we work on?

- **Before closing**: operational and IT due diligence on systems, processes, contracts, integration risks and hidden costs; an integration or separation plan ready from day one, with clear timing and responsibilities
- **Integration**: synergies on systems and licences, procurement and suppliers, administrative and logistics processes; and the full potential of the acquired business
- **Disposal and carve-out**: transitional service agreements (TSA) with deadlines under control, so you do not pay for them longer than needed; separating systems and starting up those of the new entity; data migration; supplier transition; organisation and governance of the remaining business
- **Towards exit**: systems, compliance (privacy, Italian Legislative Decree 231) and data quality

## How do we approach it?

We work on two fronts in parallel: the remaining business must be optimised, while the acquired or divested business must not stop. What matters is control of deadlines and clear communication with employees, customers and suppliers.

{{< esperienza >}}
A multinational group had decided to sell its influenza vaccines business. The commercial operations were separated across Europe and international markets: transitional service agreements with the former organisation to ensure continuity, transfer of a selected part of the staff, and temporary oversight of the business until the deal was completed. The transition closed successfully, exceeding the growth targets.
{{< /esperienza >}}

## Where do we start?

Before closing, with an operational and IT due diligence. If the deal has already closed, with a [process diagnostics](/en/process-diagnostics/) of both organisations, to see where the ways of working differ.
