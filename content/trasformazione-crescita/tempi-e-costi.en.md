## When does it help?

{{< spunta >}}
- Quotes, new product development or deliveries take too long
- The market, customers or the geo-economic context demand more speed and better prices
- New technologies are changing the way you produce
- Errors, rework and manual re-keying are multiplying
{{< /spunta >}}

## Which levers do we work on?

- **Lead time analysis** across the whole process (quoting, design, production, delivery), to remove waiting, rework and unnecessary steps
- **Independent selection of supporting systems**, such as ERP, WMS and manufacturing systems, with business case and negotiation
- **Connecting machines, instruments and sensors** to business systems, to eliminate manual re-keying and errors
- **Consolidating sites and activities**, where it pays
- **Coordinating specialised partners**

## How do we approach it?

First we define priorities and strategic choices with you; then we carefully analyse the processes and systems to be redesigned. We coordinate the specialised partners needed, drawing on long industrial experience. The focus always stays on results: shorter delivery and development times, efficiency gains on costs.

{{< esperienza >}}
A manufacturer of equipment for explosive atmospheres (ATEX) needed clarity on processes and systems before investing in a new ERP and a new WMS. The process review guided a vendor-independent selection and the negotiation of the contracts. After go-live, quotation times for engineered orders and operational errors fell by about two thirds.
{{< /esperienza >}}

## Where do we start?

Usually with a [process diagnostics](/en/process-diagnostics/), which measures where time is lost across the cycle and what it is worth.
