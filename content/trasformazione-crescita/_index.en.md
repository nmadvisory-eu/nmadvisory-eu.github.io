## Why now?

A winning strategy no longer lasts for years as it once did. Economic and geopolitical turbulence, fast-changing technology, new markets, innovations that spread at once, M&A transactions: growth has become almost a condition for staying in the market.

And transforming often means coordinating several partners: consultants, technology specialists, lawyers, financial experts.

These are not the only possible engagements. They are the ones that, in our experience inside companies and in consulting, recur across many industries and have brought concrete, lasting benefits.

## How do we approach them?

There are no standard recipes: every situation is different. We start from a careful review of the organisation and the market, agree the objectives with you, then stay through execution until the results arrive. When needed, we coordinate the work of the other specialists involved.

The first step often has a defined scope and delivers results within a few weeks: a process diagnostics, an indirect cost review or, before an acquisition, an operational and IT due diligence.

## Who leads these engagements?

The partners, in person. They have lived the same transformations as managers: growth and restructurings in international life sciences groups, carve-outs and post-acquisition integrations, new operating models in large companies. Each page describes one of these experiences, anonymously.
