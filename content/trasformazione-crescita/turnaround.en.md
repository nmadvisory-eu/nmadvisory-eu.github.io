## When does it help?

{{< spunta >}}
- The company is in serious economic or financial difficulty
- Shareholders and lenders want a credible plan, quickly
- The cuts made so far have not changed the course
{{< /spunta >}}

## Which levers do we work on?

- **Rapid diagnosis** of costs, processes, organisation and systems, to reach within a few weeks a base of facts shared with management, shareholders and lenders
- **Quick wins on costs** that do not touch the heart of the business: indirect costs, supply contracts, IT licences and services, non-core activities to outsource
- **Redesigned processes** that cost less structurally, without losing the capabilities the relaunch will need
- **Strict priorities on technology investment**, removal of redundant systems, reliable data for decisions
- **Programme governance**: a few indicators (costs, cash, service) and a tight progress cadence
- **Interim roles** for the transition period: operations, information systems, programme management

## How do we approach it?

There are no standard recipes: every situation is unique. People, systems, investments and key processes must be addressed together, almost never separately. A radical cost reduction is almost always necessary and requires a great deal of experience, especially in managing people. That is why we work alongside management, and stay present when needed.

## Where do we start?

In a turnaround, time matters. We often start with a rapid [process diagnostics](/en/process-diagnostics/) together with an [indirect cost review](/en/indirect-costs/), which delivers results within a few weeks.
